# Community conduct

Be respectful and discuss the work rather than attacking people. Help others reproduce problems, acknowledge hardware differences and make room for different experience levels.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, publishing another person's private information and spam are not acceptable. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when needed to keep the project usable.

For a sensitive conduct concern, use a private contact channel that the maintainer has explicitly published on their GitHub profile. Do not use a security advisory for an unrelated conduct complaint. If no private channel is available, avoid sharing personal details publicly; use GitHub's platform reporting tools where appropriate.
